Executive Summary

Trusted and good value — but seen as generic, and slowed by process

Employees consistently rate Pharco as trustworthy, high quality, and good value for money. But the brand is still widely perceived as an “affordable/generic” player rather than the innovator its people want it to become — and internal approval bottlenecks are the #1 obstacle to fixing it.

Three wins to protect, three risks to fix

Executive Summary

Five priorities for the next quarter

1
Fix the approval bottleneckStreamline sign-off workflow for brand materials — the fastest, most concrete win available.
2
Unify execution across Group entitiesOne shared guideline system with clear governance — inconsistency is the #1 qualitative theme (32%).
3
Reposition deliberatelyQuality and trust scores are strong (4.1 / 4.0) but 62% still see “affordable/generic” — message toward quality & innovation.
4
Close the innovation perception gapInvest in visible proof points (R&D, digital, new formats) — “Innovative” is the lowest tone score.
5
Re-engage Marketing & Medical/Scientific AffairsThese functions rate the brand most critically despite owning its execution and credibility.
